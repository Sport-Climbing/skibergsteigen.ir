SKIBERGSTEIGEN is a static website. It does not offer accounts, and it does not run its own database.

## Pages you read

The pages are files. Reading them does not create a profile on this server. This release does not embed an analytics service and does not set a cookie of its own for tracking. If that changes, this page will name the service before it is switched on.

## The inquiry form

Submitting the inquiry form sends the fields to [FormSubmit](https://formsubmit.co/), which emails them to the address published on the [contact page](/contact/). The fields are your name, email, nationality, destination, type of experience, experience level, dates, number of participants, and message. A hidden field exists to catch automated submissions. This site does not keep its own copy. FormSubmit's own terms apply to the delivery. The first time the form is used, FormSubmit asks that address to confirm delivery before a message is passed on.

## Contact details

Email, phone, and address are shown only if they have been entered in `_data/site.yml`. An empty field is described as unpublished. It is not replaced with a guess.

## Operator

The legal name of the operator is unpublished until it is entered in `_data/site.yml`. Until then, the [contact page](/contact/) is the door, and the published email address receives the inquiry.
